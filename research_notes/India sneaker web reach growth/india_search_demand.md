# India sneaker search demand, late 2026

Research date 2026-11-21. About 15 tool calls. No Google Trends, Keyword Planner, Semrush or Ahrefs data was reachable, so there are no verified per-model India search volumes. Everything below is dated and sourced; gaps are listed.

## Which models carry search demand in India (2025 and 2026)

### Takeaway

No public, verifiable ranking of India sneaker search volume by model was found. The only model-level signals are a seller blog's unsourced growth percentages, a global Farfetch figure, and marketplace price listings. Treat model priorities as directional and confirm in Google Trends (geo India) and Search Console.

### Cited Findings

- Hustle Club (Indian reseller, blog labelled 2026, author is the founder) lists growth percentages, not volumes, with no stated method or data source: Air Jordan 22 percent (Jordan 1 High/Mid/Low, Jordan 4), New Balance 48 percent (550, 9060, 1906, 530, 2002R, "Rs 8,000 to 15,000"), On Running 42 percent (Cloud 6/5, Cloudmonster), Nike Dunks 28 percent (Panda), HOKA 38 percent (Bondi, Clifton), Salomon 35 percent (XT-6), Adidas Samba and Gazelle 25 percent, ASICS 32 percent (Gel-Kayano, Gel-1130), Air Force 1 "steady" (all-white, Rs 8,000 to 10,000), Yeezy "stabilized after 2025 volatility" (Rs 20,000 to 25,000). The ranking basis is unexplained and the seller has a promotional interest. Self-reported, unverified. — [Hustle Club](https://www.hustleclub.co.in/blogs/articles/which-sneaker-brands-are-dominating-search-volume)
- Farfetch reports more than 52 million global searches for the adidas Samba in 2025 (global, not India). — [Farfetch Index](https://www.farfetch.com/stories/fashion-feed/the-farfetch-index)
- A Who What Wear piece, citing a mail-forwarding firm's Google analysis (date unclear), says the Adidas Superstar is the most-googled sneaker in 51 countries including India. Weak and undated. — [Who What Wear](https://whowhatwear.com/most-popuar-sneakers-around-the-world)
- Google's India Year in Search 2025 coverage found no sneaker entries in the retrieved excerpts. — [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/explore-communicate/indias-year-in-search-2025-the-a-to-z-of-trending-searches/)
- Flipkart FlipTrends 2025 coverage names no sneaker ranking; one syndicated article says Indians "searched for everything from shoes to iPhones". Air fryers passed 18 lakh searches (for scale of a top category). — [Tribune/PTI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fliptrends2025-gen-z-drives-scroll-led-discovery-tier-2-cities-lead-premiumisation-and-video-quick-commerce-become-indias-dominant-habits-2-2/)
- Indian price anchors seen on listings in 2026: Nike Dunk Low 'Samba' MRP Rs 8,295 (Nike India); Adidas Samba OG Rs 10,999 (adidas.co.in, discounted to about Rs 7,700 at some Nykaa Fashion sellers). — [Nike India](https://www.nike.com/in/launch/t/dunk-low-samba), [Nykaa Fashion](https://www.nykaafashion.com/adidas-samba/c/76620)
- A coupon-site guide calls the Puma Speedcat OG (Rs 9,999) the sneaker that overtook the Samba in popularity over 2025 and 2026. Low-quality affiliate source, unverified. — [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/best-sneakers-under-5000-tata-cliq-nike-adidas-puma-2026)

### Inferences

- Candidate priority list (not proven by data): Air Force 1, Samba/Gazelle, Dunk Low, AJ1 Low/High, NB 9060/530/550/1906R, Puma Speedcat, ASICS Kayano. Most are inside the Rs 8,000 to 15,000 band.
- Nothing found for Campus 00s, Mexico 66, Court Vision or Downshifter; no claim can be made.

### Gaps

- No Google Trends comparison, Keyword Planner or Semrush India volumes for any model. Must be pulled by the owner (Trends geo IN, 12 months; Search Console queries).
- Court Vision, Downshifter, Onitsuka Tiger, Campus 00s, Speedcat volumes: not found.

## Query shapes Indians use

### Takeaway

No source gave volumes by query shape. Evidence is indirect: Indian buyers see MRP versus discounted prices across marketplaces, and "best sneakers under X" listicles are abundant (so they are competitive, with Puma, Esquire India and coupon sites ranking).

### Cited Findings

- SERP evidence for "best sneakers under 5000/10000": brand pages (Puma India), Esquire India, Zoutons, Ludic, Reviehub, Magicdrop and CouponzGuru publish dated 2026 guides, so these shapes are already contested. — [Puma India](https://in.puma.com/in/en/shop/best-sneakers-under-5000), [Esquire India](https://www.esquireindia.co.in/amp/story/style/just-landed/best-sneakers-under-10000-rs), [Zoutons](https://zoutons.com/news/puma-sneakers-under-3000-5000-10000)
- Budget picks in those guides: Adidas Breaknet Rs 3,000 to 4,000 (Ludic), Puma Smashic about Rs 2,519 on Amazon India (1 July 2026, Magicdrop), Adidas Grand Court 2.0 Rs 3,999.50 (Esquire). — [Ludic](https://ludic.life/blogs/footwear/best-sneakers-under-5000-india), [Magicdrop](https://magicdrop.in/drops/best-sneakers-under-5000)
- Price confusion exists: one third-party page dated July 2026 lists a Samba OG Big Kid at Rs 17,407 on Amazon India versus Rs 10,999 adult official price. — [Magicdrop](https://magicdrop.in/drops/black-samba)

### Inferences

- "<model> price in india" is the shape the owner's project already targets; I found no competing volume data to confirm it is the largest. Under-budget listicles look high volume but crowded by media; model plus colourway plus price pages are the likely gap for a small store (inference).

### Gaps

- Volumes for: price in india, original price, first copy, original vs fake, size guide, release date india, Hindi/Hinglish variants. All not found. Autocomplete was not obtainable via these tools.

## Market size, direction, how Indians research and buy

### Takeaway

Sneaker market estimates cluster around USD 3.2 to 4.0 billion with 5 to 6 percent CAGR from analysts, while resellers and Kotak Neo quote 10 to 25 percent; definitions differ. Tier 2 and 3 cities are the majority of marketplace orders.

### Cited Findings

- IMARC (Oct 2026 edition): India sneaker market USD 3.98 billion in 2025, USD 6.53 billion by 2034, CAGR 5.65 percent for 2026 to 2034. — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-sneaker-market)
- Older IMARC edition: USD 3.88 billion FY24 to about USD 6 billion FY32, CAGR 5.45 percent (Entrepreneur India and Kotak Neo cite this). — [Kotak Neo, updated 22 May 2026](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/sneaker-market-in-india/)
- TechSci: USD 3.20 billion (2024), CAGR 5.87 percent (2025 to 2030). — [TechSci](https://www.techsciresearch.com/report/india-sneakers-market/27922.html)
- Statista: India footwear revenue USD 57.10 billion in 2026, CAGR 7.82 percent to 2031 (all footwear). — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/footwear/india/)
- Kotak Neo: sneakers 20 to 25 percent of footwear, over 70 million pairs by 2029, industry growing 12 to 15 percent (no methodology; conflicts with analyst CAGR), premium sneaker sales at Adidas and Myntra up 30 to 40 percent, Nike about 20 percent share. — [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/sneaker-market-in-india/)
- Reseller self-reports (Inc42, April 2025, unverified): Find Your Kicks 2,000 to 2,500 pairs per month, AOV Rs 10,000 to 11,000; Hustle Culture about 500 pairs per month, AOV Rs 10,000 to 13,000; Comet 12,000 customers per month, AOV Rs 4,000 to 6,000; CDC sales 50-50 online and offline; Find Your Kicks cites 10 to 11 percent CAGR; some experts "at least 25 percent". — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-sneaker-resellers-d2c-brands-are-cashing-in-on-indias-6-bn-boom/)
- Resale markups quoted are anecdotal (Rs 10,000 pair to Rs 12,000 to Rs 1,00,000; Rs 15,000 to Rs 50,000 example); no resale market size found for sneakers (a generic fashion resale report says USD 0.86 billion for 2026, not sneaker-specific). — [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/investing-guide/articles/sneaker-market-in-india/), [ReportCubes](https://www.thereportcubes.com/report-store/resale-fashion-market-india)
- Fynd Festive 2025 (Sept to Oct 2025, released 19 Nov 2025): tier 2 and 3 were 65 percent of orders, tier 3 alone 46 percent; footwear rose from 7 to 33 percent of festive sales (as reported, base year unclear); average discount fell from 44 to 34 percent; Myntra plus Flipkart share of orders fell from 89 to 76.8 percent per a later report. — [Fynd](https://www.fynd.com/press-releases/indias-e-commerce-gets-smarter-not-just-bigger-fynds-festive-report-2025-highlights-rise-of-tier-3-shoppers-and-omnichannel-growth), [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/sector/e-commerce-marketplaces/myntra-flipkarts-festive-season-fashion-order-share-falls-to-76-8)
- Flipkart: over 60 percent of festive orders from tier 2 and 3; FlipTrends 2025 says tier 2 cities lead premiumisation; creator and video-led discovery (10.3 million daily video-commerce users; a viral Y2K reel drove 21 lakh searches). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/companies/news/flipkart-festive-season-sales-tier-2-city-growth-2025-125110401438_1.html), [Tribune/PTI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/fliptrends2025-gen-z-drives-scroll-led-discovery-tier-2-cities-lead-premiumisation-and-video-quick-commerce-become-indias-dominant-habits-2-2/)
- Redseer via BW Retail World: top 10 brands are 45 percent of online footwear GMV versus 12 percent in apparel (April to June 2026). — [BW Retail World](https://bwretailworld.com/sector/fashion-lifestyle/online-footwear-market-more-concentrated-than-apparel)

### Inferences

- Brand-concentrated online footwear means model-name queries (brand plus model) are the shape that matters, and a small store competes with marketplace listings on exactly those.
- Instagram, creators and reels appear to drive discovery (Flipkart, Find Your Kicks began as an Instagram community); no survey with Google versus Instagram versus YouTube shares was found.

### Gaps

- No survey on how Indians research sneakers; no share of shoppers buying sneakers online; no tier-wise sneaker numbers.

## Seasonality

### Takeaway

Only the festive season (Sept to Nov) and Republic Day have sourced marketplace data, and neither isolates sneakers.

### Cited Findings

- Festive 2025: Flipkart Big Billion Days user visits up 21 percent in opening days versus 2024; Flipkart said carts held "the newest smartphones and sneakers" (qualitative). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/flipkart-festival-sale-2025-premium-demand-growth-125092401282_1.html)
- Fynd Republic Day 2026 report: Myntra led with over 45 percent marketplace order share; footwear demand spread across marketplaces. — [Fynd](https://fynd.com/press-releases/fynds-republic-day-report-2026-reveals-new-shopping-patterns-in-fashion-e-commerce)
- Indian Sneaker Festival is held in December (Mumbai 6 to 7 Dec 2025; Gurugram 19 to 20 Dec 2026), see above.

### Gaps

- No month-by-month sneaker search index. Air Max Day, End of Reason Sale, wedding season and back-to-college: no sourced data found.

## Budget versus hype split

### Takeaway

No numeric split exists. Evidence suggests value is concentrated Rs 8,000 to 15,000 in resale and D2C averages, while volume sits in marketplace budget picks.

### Cited Findings

- Comet D2C AOV Rs 4,000 to 6,000 versus resellers' AOV Rs 10,000 to 13,000. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/how-sneaker-resellers-d2c-brands-are-cashing-in-on-indias-6-bn-boom/)
- Sneakers replace formal shoes at work, with buyers in their 30s and 40s (same source, qualitative).
- Retail anchors: Samba OG Rs 10,999, Dunk Low Samba Rs 8,295, Speedcat OG Rs 9,999, Hustle Club's AF1 Rs 8,000 to 10,000 and Yeezy Rs 20,000 to 25,000 (seller claims).

### Inferences

- The owner's range (most pairs Rs 8,000 to 15,000) sits between marketplace budget volume and hype resale.

### Gaps

- No data on share of searches or sales under Rs 10,000 versus above Rs 15,000.
