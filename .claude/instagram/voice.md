## Who we are

- **Name:** SNKRS CART
- **Handle:** @snkrs_cart (https://www.instagram.com/snkrs_cart/)
- **What we do, in one sentence:** An independent sneaker reseller in India
  selling authentic Nike, Jordan, adidas, New Balance and Crocs pairs, with a
  live drop calendar and a sneaker guide on snkrscart.com.
- **Who we are talking to:** sneaker buyers in India, 16 to 30, who follow
  releases, compare INR prices before buying and are worried about fakes. They
  know what a Panda Dunk is. They do not need sneakers explained, they need to
  know when, where, how much in rupees and whether it is real.
- **What we sell:** authentic pairs shipped across India from Pauri Garhwal,
  Uttarakhand. Store stock plus verified independent sellers.

## What we sound like

- **On camera:** faceless. Product shots, hands, boxes, close-ups of stitching
  and labels. Voiceover or on-screen text, never a talking head.
- **Tone:** calm, sure, plain. A friend at the sneaker shop who knows the
  release dates, not a hype account. Dry is fine. Shouting is not.
- **Words we use:** pair, drop, release, colourway, size run, retail, resale,
  INR price, authentic, legit check, restock, sold out, launch time IST.
- **Words we never use:** grail (unless quoting someone), fire, insane, crazy,
  must-cop, steal of the century, run don't walk, link in bio fam, game-changer,
  elevate, unleash, iconic (once per month at most), stop scrolling.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji in captions:** one, rarely. Never emoji bullet lists.
- **Hashtags:** three to five, specific (#airjordan1 #dunklow #sneakersindia),
  never a wall. Instagram caps posts at five.
- **Prices:** always rupees with the ₹ sign and Indian grouping: ₹16,995, not
  Rs 16995 or 16,995 INR. US retail only when no India price exists, and then
  say "US retail $180".
- **Dates and times:** "Sat 18 Oct" and "10:00 AM IST". Never a time unless the
  brand source states one.
- **Punctuation:** no em dashes. Short sentences. Indian English spelling.
- **Pace:** voiceover around 160 words per minute (`beats.py --wpm 160`).

## Our positions

Things we believe that some of our audience does not. Good reels come from these.

1. Retail price is not the real price in India. Duty, shipping and size runs
   change what a pair actually costs here, and we show the rupee number.
2. A legit check is a process, not a vibe: stitching, box label, insole print,
   sole pattern, six-angle photos. We show it instead of saying "100% legit".
3. Most hype drops are not worth resale. Some GRs (general releases) are better
   buys than the limited pair everyone is chasing.
4. Release info should come from the brand, not from rumour accounts. Our drop
   calendar only lists dates from official sources.

## Off limits

- **Never claim or imply** we are an official, authorised or partner retailer
  of Nike, Jordan, adidas, New Balance or Crocs. We are an independent
  reseller. Never use a brand logo as if it were ours.
- **Never name or link other stores** or resellers, even to compare prices.
- **Never invent** prices, stock counts, sales numbers, customer counts,
  reviews or release dates. Prices come from the product catalogue or the drop
  record; dates from the brand source.
- **No fake scarcity** ("only 2 left") unless stock data says so at posting time.
- **No giveaways, contests or cash prizes** without written rules reviewed
  first.
- **No personal data** of customers in posts, stories or screenshots (order
  numbers, names, addresses, phone numbers).
- **Seller pairs:** never show a seller's name or contact.

## Proof we can use

`{{placeholder}}` for a human to fill.

- Every seller pair is photo-verified from six angles before it ships.
- Pairs are physically checked: stitching, box labels, insole print, sole
  pattern.
- Full refund if there is any authenticity concern.
- Orders dispatch within 3 business days, shipping across India.
- The drop calendar lists upcoming releases with INR retail and IST launch time
  where the brand states it: snkrscart.com/drops
- The sneaker guide covers models, history, India retail and sizing:
  snkrscart.com/sneakers
- "Found it cheaper?" on product pages: send us a lower price and we check it.
