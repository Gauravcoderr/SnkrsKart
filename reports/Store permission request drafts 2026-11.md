## Base letter

Subject: Request to show your prices on our India sneaker price pages

Hello,

[opening line for the store]

What we are asking for: your written permission to display, for sneakers you stock, your store name as plain text, your current price, the sizes in stock and a link to your own product page.

What you get: a link to your product page on every pair we cover, shown to shoppers who are searching for that model's price in India. We do not charge for this and we do not ask for commission.

How we would handle your data:

1. Text name and link only. No logo, no product photos, no copied descriptions.
2. Every price carries the date and time we last checked it, and a note asking shoppers to confirm on your site. Prices older than 48 hours are hidden.
3. We match pairs by manufacturer style code only, so your price is never shown against a different colourway.
4. If you prefer, send us a product feed or tell us which pages we may read and how often. We will follow that and your robots.txt.
5. You can ask us to correct or remove your listings at any time by replying to this email, and we will do it within one business day.

We are also a seller, so our own offer appears in the same list, labelled as ours and ranked by the same rule as everyone else (lowest price for the chosen size).

If this works for you, a one-line reply saying so is enough. If you would rather not be listed, tell us and we will leave your store out.

Thank you,
SNKRS CART
