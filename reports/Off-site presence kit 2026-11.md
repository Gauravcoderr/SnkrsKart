## Reddit (r/SneakersIndia and Indian fashion subs)

The research could not read the rules, flair or member counts (Reddit blocks fetching). Read each sub's rules in a logged-in browser before posting anything.

- Take part as a person for weeks before any store mention. Answer sizing, fit and "is this pair real" questions.
- Say you run a store whenever it is relevant. Never post a link in early comments.
- Message the moderators and ask what a store owner may post. Follow their answer.
- In "is this legit" threads, offer a free check with SneakerAuth (`legitcheck.snkrscart.com`) and post the result. Do not sell in the thread.
- Never ask friends to upvote, never use a second account.

Why it matters: a 26 October 2026 guide in The Nod built its list of Indian sneaker brands from r/SneakersIndia mentions, so Reddit chatter feeds the lists that journalists and AI answers cite.

## Never do

- No paid or gifted reviews without the incentive flag, no reviews by staff, no AI-written reviews. Google bars them and India's 2023 dark-pattern guidelines treat fake reviews as a violation.
- No customer, order or accuracy numbers that are not measured.
- No other store's name, photos or copy on our pages.
- No retry of blocked competitor sites from another IP address to get around their rate limits.
